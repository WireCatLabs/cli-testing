Rules. The sheet answers questions Q1-Q30 and variants V3a, V3b, V9a, V9b, V12a and V12b. For each question use the
key's checklist points and its scoring line: 1 = every checklist point present and the record(s) it comes from named
(a deal, person, company, note or task, by name or id); 0.5 = the main fact present but a point missing, or no record
named; 0 = not found or wrong. Where the key says what an answer that asks back or lists several candidates scores,
use that. Mark CW (confidently wrong) when the sheet asserts a wrong fact without a hedge, or gives an answer to a
question (or half of one) the key says has no answer in the data. Extra true facts never lower a score. Names may be
written with or without the " (WC seed)" suffix. Amounts may be written in any format or currency sign; times may be
shown in any time zone when correct.
Layout: a table with one row per question Q1-Q30 (score, CW or blank, a short quote), the total of 30 and the number
of CW; then a second table for the six variants with their total of 6.
