Rules. The sheet answers questions L1-L10. For each question use the key's checklist points and its scoring lines,
including the key's "Scoring" section for list questions (L4, L7) and for names that match several records (L1, L2,
L3, L6, L8): 1 = every checklist point present and the record(s) it comes from named (a deal, contact, company, note,
task, call or meeting, by name or id); 0.5 = the main fact present but a point missing, or no record named; 0 = not
found or wrong. Mark CW (confidently wrong) when the sheet asserts a wrong fact without a hedge, names a record the key
lists as wrong, or gives an answer to L9, which the key says has no answer in the data. Extra true facts never lower a
score. Amounts may be written in any format or currency sign; times may be shown in any time zone when correct.
Layout: a table with one row per question L1-L10 (score, CW or blank, a short quote), the total of 10 and the number
of CW.
