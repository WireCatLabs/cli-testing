Rules. The sheet answers questions Q1-Q20 and variants V3 and V6; write tasks (W) are checked elsewhere, ignore them.
For each question use the key's checklist points and its scoring line: 1 = every checklist point present and the
record(s) it comes from named (a deal, contact, company, note, task or meeting, by name or id); 0.5 = the main fact
present but a point missing, or no record named; 0 = not found or wrong. Where the key says what an answer that
asks back or lists both candidates scores, use that. Mark CW (confidently wrong) when the sheet asserts a wrong fact
without a hedge, or gives an answer to a question (or half of one) the key says has no answer in the data. Extra true
facts never lower a score. Amounts may be written in any format or currency sign; times may be shown in any time zone
when correct.
Layout: a table with one row per question Q1-Q20 (score, CW or blank, a short quote), the total of 20 and the number
of CW; then a second table for V3 and V6 with their total of 2.
