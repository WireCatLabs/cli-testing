You label pairs of tasks that an agent drafted from the owner's chat. Decide whether the two tasks are the SAME JOB
(one should be merged into the other) or DIFFERENT JOBS. Read the conversation around both messages.

SAME: the same concrete outcome, still the same pending thing: a reminder or chase of the same request, the same
promise repeated, the same meeting moved to another time, the follow-through of the same ask, or one task is only a
part or restatement of the other.
DIFFERENT: different deliverables or topics; different instances of a recurring thing (weekly meetings in different
weeks, two payments, two batches); consecutive steps (ask for X vs review X after it arrived, estimate vs decide);
a different actor doing the work; two jobs taken from one message; the earlier job was finished before the later one
came up.
UNSURE: only when the conversation truly does not let you tell. Do not guess SAME or DIFFERENT when unsure.

{"label": "same" | "different" | "unsure", "reason": "<one plain English sentence, no names of people>"}
